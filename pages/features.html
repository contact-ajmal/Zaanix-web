The platform

Everything a data team does, on one DuckDB workspace.

ZAANIX runs a DuckDB engine for each workspace inside its own server. Around it: the tools to connect data, query and model it, build what people use, and govern who sees what — for people, AI and agents alike.

Connect

35 kinds of source, connected once and used by every workspace. Credentials are encrypted and write-only.

Connections and syncs
Object storageAmazon S3, Cloudflare R2, Google Cloud Storage and Azure Blob — Parquet, CSV, JSON, Delta and Iceberg, queried in place.
Lakehouse catalogsAWS Glue, S3 Tables, any Iceberg REST catalog (Polaris, Nessie, Lakekeeper) and Databricks Unity Catalog.
DatabasesPostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite and other DuckDB files, attached read-only.
WarehousesSnowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, ClickHouse and Microsoft Fabric.
SaaS applicationsSalesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, GA4, Airtable, Notion, GitHub, Jira, Zendesk, Shopify, Intercom, Linear, Pipedrive and Mailchimp.
Google Drive and SheetsSign in with a Google account, or use a public Sheets link; HTTP and REST endpoints too.
Scheduled syncsFull, incremental or append loads on a schedule, with transformations checked before they save.
Streams and change data captureKafka topics, Kinesis streams and HTTP pushes appended as events arrive; Postgres tables mirrored through logical replication, or Debezium events.
Reverse ETLQuery results sent to databases, files or APIs — only what changed — and written back to Iceberg and Delta tables.
Workspaces anywhereA workspace is a DuckDB file in any folder on the server, or an object in S3, R2, GCS or Azure kept in sync.

Model and trust

Definitions people agree on, and checks that say when the data is wrong.

Models, metrics and quality
dbt projectsReal dbt Core compiles against a shadow of the workspace; ZAANIX runs the SQL in the workspace's engine. Save any query as a model.
Semantic layerMetrics defined once (MetricFlow-shaped, imported from dbt) and computed the same way in the explorer, by the AI and by agents.
Questions from metricsAsk "revenue by region last quarter" and the answer is computed through the semantic layer, not guessed.
Data qualityChecks suggested from the data — nulls, duplicates, categories, ranges, joins, freshness — on a schedule, with the failing rows one click away.
Metric monitorsUnusual days, weeks and months flagged, with the segments that drove the change, delivered to a channel.
Schema and freshness watchesKnow when a table's columns change or its data stops arriving.
Prepare dataStep-by-step recipes — filter, rename, cast, fill, clean text, split, deduplicate — previewed step by step and saved as a view, a table or a dbt model.
Find joinsHow tables join — declared keys and relationships inferred from names — confirmed on the data: coverage and the rows an inner join would drop.
